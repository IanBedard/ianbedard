The goal

Physio Rivière’s old site was English-only, hard to use on phones, and didn’t show up well in local searches. Many patients have limited mobility or low vision, so accessibility mattered.

The clinic wanted patients to find it on Google, understand its services and book an assessment in a few taps.

02

What I built

  • 8-page bilingual site (FR/EN): services, team, pricing and direct billing, FAQ and contact
  • WCAG 2.2 AA: large text, strong contrast, works fully with a keyboard and screen reader
  • Online booking button connected to the clinic’s existing booking system
  • Google Business Profile set up with hours, services, photos and both languages
  • Basic SEO: page titles, descriptions and local business information
  • Contact form that sends requests straight to the front desk
