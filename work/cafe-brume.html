Café Brume

A bilingual online shop for a small-batch coffee roaster, with recurring subscriptions and local pickup.

Type
Shopify store
Business
Coffee roaster, Gatineau
Package
Shop Launch + Subscriptions add-on

The goal

Café Brume roasts coffee in small batches and sells it at its café and at weekend markets. It wanted to sell online to customers on both sides of the river, without hiring staff to manage a store.

The store had to work equally well in French and English, make subscriptions simple, and stay easy for the owners to update themselves.

What I built

  • Bilingual store (FR/EN) with translated products, checkout, emails and policies
  • Coffee subscriptions: every 2 or 4 weeks, at 10% off, which customers can pause or skip
  • Accessible checkout: keyboard-friendly, clear error messages, good colour contrast
  • Local pickup at the café, plus shipping across Canada with taxes set up for QC and ON
  • Product filters by roast, origin and grind
  • A 1-hour training session so the owners can add new coffees themselves
