The goal

Café Brume roasts seasonal single origins and house blends in small batches every Tuesday. It needed an online store that felt as calm and considered as its coffee, and that turned one-time buyers into regulars.

The store had to make subscriptions effortless, help new customers pick the right roast, and be usable by everyone, on any device.

What I built

  • Subscribe and save 10%, delivered every 2, 3 or 4 weeks, with free shipping. Customers can pause, skip or cancel from their account
  • Product pages with tasting notes, origin and process, size and grind options, and a subscribe-or-buy-once choice
  • “Find your roast” paths (light, medium, dark, decaf) for customers who aren’t sure where to start
  • Brew guides for pour-over, French press and moka pot
  • Accessible to everyone: built to the WCAG 2.2 AA standard, with an accessibility statement and a way to ask for help
  • Bilingual touches in the announcement bar and section labels (FR/EN)
  • Custom theme designed in Figma, then built in Shopify
