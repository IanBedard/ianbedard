FAQ

Common questions

How long does a project take?

A Brand Site usually takes 3–4 weeks and a Shop Launch 4–6 weeks, depending on how quickly content and feedback come in. Need it sooner? Rush delivery (under 2 weeks) is available for +20%.

How do payments work?

A 50% deposit reserves your spot and starts the project, and the other 50% is due at launch. For projects over $4,000, it’s split 40% to start, 30% at the design stage and 30% at launch.

Who pays for Shopify, the domain and apps?

You do, directly. Your Shopify plan, domain name and any paid apps are in your name and on your card, so you fully own your store and site. I’ll help you pick the right plan and set everything up.

How many revisions do I get?

Brand Site and Shop Launch include 2 rounds of revisions. Additional rounds or new features are quoted up front, so there are never surprise bills.

Do I need to write the content in both languages?

You can provide text in one or both languages. I can also write it for you with the Copywriting add-on ($100/page).

What does “accessible” mean for my site?

Your site follows the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2 AA). People using screen readers, keyboards, magnification or older devices can still read your content and buy from you.
